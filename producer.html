What I make

01

Original Production

Club records and tracks written to brief. Afro house, tech house, melodic and Latin-leaning. Delivered mixed, with stems.

02

Remixes & Edits

Official remixes and private edits. Reworks that keep the song and move the room.

03

Sonic Identity

A sound for the brand: signature palette, stings, in-store and event music, delivered as a system, not a playlist.

04

Venue Programming

Curated, hour-by-hour music programs for restaurants, hotels and members' spaces. Built, licensed guidance, and refreshed by season.

03 / 04

How it runs

  1. Brief

    Room, audience, hours, references. One call.

  2. Sketch

    Two or three directions within a week. You pick one.

  3. Build

    Full production in Ableton Live. Two rounds of notes included.

  4. Deliver

    Masters, stems and alt versions. Radio, club and edit lengths as needed.
